## OVERVIEW
This is an AI integrated web-app that allows E-com business owners to generate captions and headlines for their products to post online.

## Upcoming updates

### 1. Site integration
The business who build their landing page from [coderadi](https://coderadi.in) can integrate their page in the application and it will automatically detect all products, their pricing and description. After after that, captions and headlines generation is just a task of **one click**.
