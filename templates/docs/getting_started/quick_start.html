Quick-start

Let's see the fastest path from creating your ECHO account and generating first AI-content.

Account creation

When you launch the app for the first time, you'll get to the signup page. Add your details to the relevant input field and click signup.

If your credentials are valid, you'll get to the dashboard, which looks something like this;

Tool selection

Now when you're on the dashboard, click the 4-dot icon in the top-right section of the screen on any device which you're on.

And then click the captions tool. Now you'll get to the captions page, here you can generation captions for social media posts and website listing.

AI generation

In the captions window, you'll see a prompt-area with product name, price, and description input. Fill out only name and description of any product you like, or a dummy product. You can copy a dummy product info from below;

TITLE: Titanium visiting card DESCRIPTION: Ultra-premium titanium cards with a satin finish — extremely durable and unique in hand-feel.

Now click the send button, and wait for a few seconds. The generation time depends on your internet speed.

    After waiting for a few seconds, you'll have your first AI content generated.

Checking for changes

Now get to your dashboard and see if the data has been changed or not, if it's changed that means it's working, if it doesn't change, that means you've got a generation for free, enjoy! 🥳
