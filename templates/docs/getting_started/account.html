Account

Let's have a quick go-through of our account features.

Basic 01

Basic Account Information

Here you can view your basic account information like your name, email, and generation. You can view your basic credits information and all generations you've saved.

There's nothing more to explain about your basic account information. The UI is good enough for you to understand everything, it's simple.

Actions 01

Account Actions

Let's see some account actions you can take right away from your account page.

1. Clear History

You can clear all of your generation history right from your account page. This may help you to keep your account clean by clearing past history, we don't know if it makes sense, we don't even know why this thing exists. It was not even in our original design. Our developers add this just because they were unemployed.

By the way, we've added a confirmation prompt so that you don't accidentally clear your history.

2. Account deletion

You can delete your account any time. This will remove all your data and settings from our servers. Do only if you're leaving ECHO, because all of your data will be deleted, including your subscriptions and generations.

Actions 02

Store Integration

You can integrate & update your store URL from your account too. Sometimes, you may see an error even by entering the right URL, like this;

You can see, we're getting error even after entering a proper URL `mystore.coderadi.in`. To fix this just add `https://` at the beginning of the URL.

For instance, instead of `mystore.coderadi.in`, use `https://mystore.coderadi.in`.
